Impact Partners

Our Beneficiaries

Direct Beneficiary

Phoenix Children's Hospital

Providing specialized CareKits, sensory toys, craft items, gift cards, and emotional encouragement to young pediatric patients enduring long-term hospital stays.

✓ Receives 100% of PCH Bake Sale Proceeds & Toy Drives
Family Support

Ronald McDonald House Charities

Supporting families traveling far from home for their child's care with pantry snacks, travel toiletries, household items, and essential comfort packages.

✓ Provides Essential Supplies for Patient Families

Our initiative hosts neighborhood bake sales and drives in accessible public spaces such as local libraries and community centers. Members can run bake sales, assemble CareKits, and earn verified volunteer hours.
