Bringing Hope & Support to Hospitalized Youth & Their Families.

Medical Miracles: Act for Impact is a student-led initiative run by high schoolers dedicated to supporting long-term hospitalized children and teens, as well as providing essential care and relief for their families during treatment.

🎓 Open to Everyone Ages 12+

Strongly encouraging all middle and high school students to join—no medical or pre-med background required! Help make an impact and gain volunteer hours.
