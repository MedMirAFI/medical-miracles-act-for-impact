Student-Led Initiative Run by High Schoolers

Bringing Hope & Support to Hospitalized Youth & Their Families.

Medical Miracles: Act for Impact is a student-led initiative run by high schoolers dedicated to supporting long-term hospitalized children and teens, as well as providing essential care and relief for their families during treatment.

Open to Everyone Ages 12+

Strongly encouraging all middle and high school students to join—no medical or pre-med background required! Help make an impact and gain volunteer hours.

Supporting Two Major Beneficiaries

Proceeds, care packages, and donations directly benefit patients at Phoenix Children's Hospital and patient families staying at Ronald McDonald House Charities.

Our Impact Partners

Who We Support

Every donation and community initiative directly helps hospitalized youth and their supporting families.

Direct Beneficiary

Phoenix Children's Hospital

Providing specialized CareKits, sensory toys, craft items, gift cards, and emotional encouragement to young pediatric patients enduring long-term hospital stays and medical treatments.

Receives 100% of PCH Bake Sale Proceeds & Toy Drives
Family Support Beneficiary

Ronald McDonald House Charities

Supporting families who travel far from home for their child's medical care. We provide essential pantry snacks, travel-size toiletries, household supplies, and essential comfort packages for patient families staying at the House.

Community Engagement

Bake Sales & Local Public Drives

Our initiative hosts neighborhood bake sales and drives in accessible, free public spaces such as local libraries and community centers. Members can actively participate in running bake sales, assembling CareKits, spreading awareness, and earning volunteer hours while supporting hospitalized youth and their families.
